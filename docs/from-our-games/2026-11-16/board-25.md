**Result** · 5♦ by North, down two. East–West vulnerable. Human opponents.

## Make the decision

South holds ♠3 ♥AJ63 ♦QJ832 ♣KJ2. North opens 1♦ and East passes. Decide what you would bid and what you expect partner to infer from it.

<details>
<summary>Reveal the auction and the comparison</summary>

With opponents passing throughout, North–South bid **1♦ → 3♦ → 4♦ → 5♦**. The packet records a **5–8 HCP** explanation for 3♦. The standard SAYC limit raise is about **10–12 points**. Your first discussion is what you both intended 3♦ to mean at this table.

South had 12 HCP and four hearts. A 1♥ response shows the major first and gives North a chance to describe strength and shape. North held ♠K82 ♥954 ♦AK94 ♣A54, so a balanced 1NT rebid is one possible continuation. Once North raises to 4♦ in the recorded auction, the partnership has passed 3NT.

Seven of the eight pairs in 5♦ went down. All three pairs who reached 3NT made it. The traveller records the final contracts and results, so the 1♥ route is an illustrative auction on your deal.
