# SAYC overcall demonstration hands

[Overcall cheat sheet](overcall-cheat-sheet.md) · [BBO Handviewer examples](overcall-handviewer.md)

North deals and neither side is vulnerable on all six deals. Calls run clockwise from North. **East overcalls, West advances.** `P` means pass and `T` means ten. Each auction stops at the teaching point.

Read the hand and the question first. Then open the explanation or bring up the full deal in Handviewer.

## 1. Raise partner's overcall under pressure

**Auction:** `1D - 1H - 1S - ?`

**West:** ♠ 7 · ♥ KT84 · ♦ 973 · ♣ Q8642 — **5 HCP**

What changes when you have four hearts and a singleton in the suit South just bid?

<details>
<summary>Reveal the bid and explanation</summary>
<p><strong>3H</strong>, using the weak jump-raise agreement. North introduces diamonds, East overcalls with five good hearts, and South shows spades. West can count at least nine hearts between the two hands. The singleton spade gives West ruffing potential if hearts become trumps. Bidding 3H makes the opponents go to the three level to bid spades again.</p>
<p>With just three hearts and a flatter hand, 2H would be the more economical raise. At unfavourable vulnerability, be more selective about jumping.</p>
</details>

## 2. Cue-bid with a stronger raise

**Auction:** `1D - 1H - P - ?`

**West:** ♠ K72 · ♥ KT84 · ♦ 973 · ♣ A86 — **10 HCP**  
**East:** ♠ 84 · ♥ AQJ96 · ♦ 84 · ♣ J975 — **8 HCP**

West has four-card support again. How can West ask whether East has enough strength to explore game?

<details>
<summary>Reveal the bid and explanation</summary>
<p><strong>2D</strong>, cue-bidding North's opening suit. The cue bid forces East to answer. After North passes, East rebids <strong>2H</strong> to show a minimum. West can stop there if South passes. The teaching sequence is <code>1D - 1H - P - 2D - P - 2H</code>.</p>
</details>

## 3. Make a weak jump overcall

**Auction:** `1D - ?`

**East:** ♠ KQJT86 · ♥ 84 · ♦ 73 · ♣ 942 — **6 HCP**

Which call describes this hand better, `1S` or `2S`?

<details>
<summary>Reveal the bid and explanation</summary>
<p><strong>2S</strong>. East has six good spades and weak-two values. Skipping 1S shows the weak jump-overcall hand and takes away the opponents' one-level bidding room.</p>
</details>

## 4. Overcall 1NT and use Stayman

**Auction:** `1D - 1NT - P - ?`

**East:** ♠ AQ84 · ♥ K7 · ♦ KJ6 · ♣ K864 — **16 HCP**  
**West:** ♠ KJ73 · ♥ Q64 · ♦ 842 · ♣ Q72 — **8 HCP**

East is balanced with a diamond stopper. How does West look for a spade fit?

<details>
<summary>Reveal the bid and explanation</summary>
<p><strong>2C</strong> is Stayman. After North passes, East answers <strong>2S</strong>, showing four spades. The pair has located an eight-card fit. The teaching sequence is <code>1D - 1NT - P - 2C - P - 2S</code>. In booklet SAYC, Stayman is the only conventional response to this 1NT overcall.</p>
</details>

## 5. Show both majors with Michaels

**Auction:** `1D - ?`

**East:** ♠ KQJ84 · ♥ AJT96 · ♦ 7 · ♣ 84 — **11 HCP**  
**West:** ♠ 72 · ♥ Q843 · ♦ 964 · ♣ K762 — **5 HCP**

Which bid tells West about both majors immediately?

<details>
<summary>Reveal the bid and explanation</summary>
<p><strong>2D</strong> is Michaels over the diamond opening. It shows at least five hearts and five spades. After South passes, West chooses <strong>2H</strong> with four hearts and two spades. The teaching sequence is <code>1D - 2D - P - 2H</code>.</p>
</details>

## 6. Show the lower unbid suits with unusual 2NT

**Auction:** `1H - ?`

**East:** ♠ 84 · ♥ 7 · ♦ KQJ96 · ♣ AJT84 — **11 HCP**  
**West:** ♠ Q732 · ♥ 964 · ♦ 84 · ♣ Q762 — **4 HCP**

Which two suits does a direct jump to `2NT` show here, and which one should West choose after South passes?

<details>
<summary>Reveal the bid and explanation</summary>
<p><strong>2NT</strong> shows clubs and diamonds, the two lowest unbid suits. East has five cards in each. West prefers <strong>3C</strong> with four clubs and two diamonds. The teaching sequence is <code>1H - 2NT - P - 3C</code>.</p>
</details>
