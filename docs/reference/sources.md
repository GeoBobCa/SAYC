## Classroom opening agreement { #classroom-agreement }

In the classroom version, **12 total points may be enough to open one of a suit** when the hand is suitable. The reference pages use the booklet's stated ranges where applicable. Apply the classroom opening agreement when practising with a partner who uses it, and disclose partnership agreements according to the event's procedures.
