# Michaels cuebids

A direct Michaels cuebid shows two long suits in one call. Use the suit the opponent opened to work out which suits the cuebid promises.

## Read the auction

The opponent opens one of a suit. You are next to act. The SAYC Yellow Card shows **at least five cards in each of the two suits**.

| Opponent opens | You bid | Your two suits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1♣ | 2♣ | Hearts and spades |
| 1♦ | 2♦ | Hearts and spades |
| 1♥ | 2♥ | Spades and an unspecified minor |
| 1♠ | 2♠ | Hearts and an unspecified minor |

## Follow the bid stream

- **1♦ → 2♦ → Pass → 2♥.** The 2♦ bidder shows both majors. Their partner chooses hearts at the two level.
- **1♥ → 2♥ → Pass → 2♠.** The 2♥ bidder shows spades and a minor. Their partner chooses the known major.
- **1♠ → 2♠ → Pass → 2NT.** The 2♠ bidder shows hearts and a minor. Their partner’s 2NT asks which minor. A 3♣ or 3♦ reply identifies it.

Before intervening, consider vulnerability, suit quality and how high partner may have to bid to choose a suit. Use the whole auction to identify a cuebid. A direct cuebid of opener’s suit and a cuebid by the overcaller’s partner arise in different auctions and have different meanings.

**Quick check.** After (1♥)–2♥–(Pass), you want to find partner’s minor. What do you bid?

<summary>Reveal</summary>
<p>2NT asks partner to name the minor. Spades are already known.</p>
