# One of a major

## Major-suit raises { #major-raise-responses }

| Response to 1♥ or 1♠ | Meaning in an uncontested auction |
| --- | --- |
| 2 of opener's major | 3+ trumps, 6–10 dummy points |
| 3 of opener's major | Limit raise, 3+ trumps and 10–11 dummy points |
| 2NT | Jacoby game-forcing raise, 13+ dummy points and normally four or more trumps |
| 4 of opener's major | Usually five or more trumps, a singleton or void, and under 10 HCP |

The booklet's simple and limit raise ranges meet at 10 dummy points. Use judgment about trump quality and useful shortness at that boundary.

## Responses without an immediate raise { #major-new-suit-responses }

| After 1♥ | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1♠ | 4+ spades and 6+ points. One-round force by an unpassed hand |
| 1NT | 6–9 points, denies four spades and three hearts. Opener may pass |
| 2♣ or 2♦ | 10+ points and at least four cards in the suit |
| 2♠, 3♣ or 3♦ | Strong jump shift with slam interest |
| 3NT | Balanced 15–17 HCP with two-card heart support |

Over 1♠ a natural 2♥ response shows at least five hearts. The [two-level response and rebid rules](rebids.md#two-level-response) apply. At the one level, 1♠ is available over 1♥.

## Jacoby 2NT continuations { #jacoby-two-notrump }

After 1♥ – 2NT, opener can show a singleton or void by bidding 3♣, 3♦ or 3♠. A new suit at the four level shows a second suit. With no shortness to show, 4♥ shows a minimum, 3NT shows about 15–17, and 3♥ shows 18+. Apply the same structure with spades agreed. Responder can settle in game, cue-bid with slam interest or use [Blackwood](slam.md#blackwood).
