# Rebids and continuations

## Opener's first rebid { #opener-rebids }

The booklet groups opener's strength approximately as follows. Pick the rebid that also describes shape and fit.

| Strength | Common descriptions |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum, 13–15 points | Cheapest natural notrump rebid, simple raise of responder, cheap rebid of opener's suit, or affordable new suit |
| Medium, 16–18 points | Jump raise, jump rebid of own suit, or a reverse with the right shape. A non-reversing new suit can span 13–18 |
| Maximum, about 19–21 or 22 points | Strong action such as a jump shift, a double jump in a known suit, or a jump in notrump with balanced shape |

After 1♣ – 1♥, a 1NT rebid is typically balanced with about 12–14 HCP and without the four-card heart support normally needed for a raise. With 15–17 balanced, opener would usually have opened 1NT. A jump to 2NT typically shows 18–19 balanced. These balanced HCP ranges are more specific than the booklet's broad strength groupings.

## Reverses and jumps { #reverses-and-jumps }

- **Reverse.** After 1♦ – 1♠, 2♥ shows extra strength and usually at least five diamonds and four hearts. Returning to diamonds now costs responder the three level. Responder keeps the auction open for one round.
- **Jump rebid of own suit.** After 1♥ – 1♠, 3♥ shows extra strength and a long heart suit. It invites game.
- **Opener's jump shift.** After 1♥ – 1♠, 3♣ introduces clubs with a very strong hand and forces to game.

## Responder's second call { #responder-second-call }

| Intention after a one-level response | Typical action, if available |
| --- | --- |
| Stop in a partscore | Pass, bid 1NT, or return to a known suit at the two level |
| Invite game | Bid 2NT or jump to three of a known suit |
| Keep exploring after opener rebids a suit | Bid a new suit. The fourth suit may be artificial and can be a one-round force in SAYC |
| Place the contract in game | Bid 3NT, 4♥, 4♠, 5♣ or 5♦ as appropriate |

After opener rebids **1NT**, a cheap new suit can be a sign-off, as in 1♥ – 1♠ – 1NT – 2♣. A reverse or jump shift by responder is game forcing, as in 1♣ – 1♥ – 1NT – 2♠ or 3♦.

## After a two-level new-suit response { #two-level-response }

An unpassed responder who introduces a new suit at the two level promises another call unless opener rebids at game. After 1♠ – 2♣ – 2♥, 2♠ is a nonforcing preference, 2NT is invitational, and 3♠ is game forcing. A direct 1♠ – 3♠ would have been a limit raise.

## A complete auction { #four-stage-example }

| Stage | Bid | Message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduce | 1♥ | Opening strength and at least five hearts |
| Explore | 1♠ | At least four spades and enough strength to respond |
| Clarify | 2♦ | A second suit. Strength may still be minimum or medium |
| Decide | 3♥ | An invitational heart raise after first showing spades |
