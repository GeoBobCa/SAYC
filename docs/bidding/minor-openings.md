# One of a minor

## Minor length and raises { #minor-length-and-raises }

A 1♣ opening can be on three clubs. A 1♦ opening usually has at least four diamonds. The three-diamond exception is 4♠–4♥–3♦–2♣. Normally have at least five clubs to raise 1♣ and four diamonds to raise 1♦. In competition, one fewer may suffice. Direct jump raises in an uncontested auction are limit raises, about 10–12 points. SAYC has no forcing direct minor raise.

## Looking for a major fit { #major-over-minor }

With 6+ points and a four-card major available at the one level, show the major when choosing between it and a routine minor raise. One-level responses generally proceed up the line. With four hearts and four clubs after 1♣, bid 1♥ if strong enough to respond. Opener may have four hearts, and club support can be investigated later if needed.

## Notrump responses { #minor-notrump-responses }

| Direct response | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1NT | About 6–10 HCP, typically no four-card major available to show |
| 2NT | Balanced 13–15 HCP, game forcing |
| 3NT | Balanced 16–18 HCP |

A direct 2NT response to a minor and [Jacoby 2NT over a major](major-openings.md#jacoby-two-notrump) have different meanings.
