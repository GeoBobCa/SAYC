# Slam bidding

## Blackwood { #blackwood }

In a Blackwood auction, 4NT asks for aces. The asker can continue with 5NT to ask for kings, confirming that the partnership has all four aces.

| Aces after 4NT | Kings after 5NT |
| --- | --- |
| 5♣ shows 0 or 4 | 6♣ shows 0 or 4 |
| 5♦ shows 1 | 6♦ shows 1 |
| 5♥ shows 2 | 6♥ shows 2 |
| 5♠ shows 3 | 6♠ shows 3 |

## Gerber { #gerber }

4♣ over partner's 1NT or 2NT, including a natural rebid, asks for aces. The replies 4♦, 4♥, 4♠ and 4NT show 0 or 4, 1, 2 and 3 aces in order. A continuation of 5♣ asks for kings using the corresponding steps at the five level and confirms all four aces. Another bid by the asker places the contract.

## Quantitative 4NT { #quantitative-four-notrump }

A direct 4NT raise of a 1NT or 2NT opening invites 6NT based on combined strength. A minimum may pass. A maximum can accept.

## Interference and Grand Slam Force { #slam-interference-and-grand-slam-force }

The Yellow Card lists DOPI after interference over an ace ask. Double shows zero, pass shows one, and the cheapest available suit shows two, continuing by steps. The convention card gives the shorthand for this agreement.

A jump to 5NT in the Grand Slam Force context asks about the agreed trump suit's top three honors. Bid seven of that suit with two of A, K and Q. Otherwise bid six. The auction distinguishes this from 5NT asking for kings after Blackwood.
