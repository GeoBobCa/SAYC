# Strong 2♣

## Opening and responses { #strong-two-clubs-responses }

The artificial 2♣ opening shows 22+ points or equivalent playing strength.

| Response | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 2♦ | Artificial waiting bid. It can include values without a suitable positive response |
| 2♥, 2♠, 3♣ or 3♦ | Natural positive, at least five cards and 8+ points. Game forcing |
| 2NT | Balanced positive. The booklet describes it as a balanced 8 HCP |

## Opener's rebid after 2♦ { #strong-two-clubs-rebids }

2NT shows 22–24 balanced. Use 3♣ Stayman, 3♦/3♥ transfers, 4♣ Gerber and quantitative 4NT, as over [a 2NT opening](notrump.md#higher-notrump-openings).

If opener rebids a suit after the waiting response, the auction is forcing through three of opener's major or four of opener's minor. For example, 2♣ – 2♦ – 2♥ – 2♠ – 3♥ may stop in 3♥.
