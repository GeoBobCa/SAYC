# Notrump openings

## Responding to 1NT { #one-notrump }

A 1NT opening shows 15–17 HCP and a balanced hand. With a hand that belongs in notrump, pass to stop, raise to 2NT to invite game, or raise to 3NT to play. A direct 4NT invites 6NT.

## Stayman { #stayman }

2♣ asks for a four-card major. Opener bids 2♦ without one, 2♥ with four hearts (including with both majors), or 2♠ with four spades and fewer than four hearts. The auction can stop at the two level. A three-level minor rebid by responder after Stayman shows at least five cards in that minor and slam interest.

## Jacoby transfers { #jacoby-transfers }

2♦ shows five or more hearts and normally requests 2♥. 2♥ shows five or more spades and normally requests 2♠. With 17 points and four-card support, opener may jump to three of the major.

| Responder's rebid after 1NT – 2♥ – 2♠ | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pass | Play 2♠ |
| 2NT or 3♠ | Invite game |
| 3♣, 3♦ or 3♥ | Natural and game forcing |
| 3NT | Offer opener a choice of 3NT or 4♠ |
| 4♠ | Play 4♠, with six or more spades |

For example, 1NT – 2♦ – 2♥ – 3NT shows a balanced 15–17 in opener's hand, five or more hearts in responder's hand, routine transfer acceptance, and then a choice of games. Opener normally chooses 4♥ with three or more hearts.

## Minor sign-offs and direct jumps { #minor-signoffs }

A direct 2♠ response requests 3♣. Pass with a weak club hand or bid 3♦ to play there. A direct 3♣ or 3♦ shows a six-card or longer suit and invites 3NT. A direct 3♥ or 3♠ shows six or more in the major and slam interest. Direct 4♣ is [Gerber](slam.md#gerber).

## Interference over 1NT { #notrump-interference }

- Over a double of 1NT, Stayman and transfers still apply.
- Over a suit overcall of 1NT, responses are natural. A cuebid can serve as game-forcing Stayman.
- If the opponents intervene over the conventional response itself, bids retain their meanings. A voluntary acceptance shows a fit. After 1NT – (Pass) – 2♦ – (Double), opener can pass with only two hearts.

## 2NT and 3NT openings { #higher-notrump-openings }

| Opening | Range | Stayman | Transfers to hearts and spades |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2NT | 20–21 HCP | 3♣ | 3♦ and 3♥ |
| 3NT | 25–27 HCP | 4♣ | 4♦ and 4♥ |

Over 2NT, direct 4♣ is Gerber and direct 4NT is quantitative. Over a 3NT opening, 4♣ is Stayman.
