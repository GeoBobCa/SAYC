# Using a ruff as an entry

An entry is a way to move the lead to the hand that needs it. In a suit contract, you can sometimes reach that hand by leading a side suit it is void in and ruffing.

## First principles

1. Count the tricks you can still afford to lose.
2. Identify the hand with the winners you need to cash.
3. Count opposing trumps. If a defender can overruff, check whether your ruff can be made safely.
4. Lead a side suit you can ruff in the hand you need to reach.

[Review how a trick is won](../../play/trick-play.md#following-suit)

## Compare two ruffs on the same deal

| Decision | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **Trick five.** Dummy leads a diamond. | West plays after South and may be void. Ruff high enough to prevent an overruff. |
| **Trick nine in the alternative line.** Dummy leads a club. | All opposing trumps are gone, so even South’s lowest trump is a safe entry. |

## Try a demo

Pause after dummy wins the ♦K at trick four. If you lead another diamond, choose which spade to ruff with. Then compare the two replays and identify how South gets back to hand in the alternative line.
