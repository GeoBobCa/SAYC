# Beginner Bridge Cheat Sheet — 1♥ Opening, 1♠ Response

**System:** SAYC, modified to permit opening a suit with **12 total points**. No opponent has bid.

## What do we know already?

- **Opener: 1♥** — normally **5+ hearts**, at least **12 total points** under our agreement.
- **Responder: 1♠** — **4+ spades** and **6+ points**. **Forcing one round:** opener must bid again.
- For **notrump** bids, count **high-card points (HCP)**. For suit bids, distribution matters too.

## Opener's rebid — how to choose

| Opener rebids | What it tells responder | Why opener chooses it |
|---|---|---|
| **2♠** | Usually **4 spades**, **12–15** total points | Found an 8-card spade fit; minimum-strength raise |
| **3♠** | **4+ spades**, about **16–18** total/support points | Found a spade fit and invites game |
| **4♠** | **4+ spades**, a very strong hand (roughly **19+** points) | Knows game should be played in spades |
| **1NT** | **12–14 HCP**, fairly balanced; normally no 4-card spade fit | No suitable suit raise or second suit; minimum balanced hand |
| **2♥** | Usually **6+ hearts**, about **12–15** total points | Minimum hand; extra length in opening suit |
| **2♣ / 2♦** | **4+ cards** in that minor, about **12–18** total points | Shows second suit; strength still a wide range |
| **2NT** | **18–19 HCP**, balanced | Strong balanced hand (too strong for 1NT rebid) |
| **3♥** | **6+ hearts**, about **16–18** total points | Stronger hand with a long heart suit; invites game |
| **3♣ / 3♦** | Strong jump shift, generally **19+** points and a real second suit | Shows a powerful two-suited hand; forces to game |

*These are practical beginner ranges. A few unusual hand shapes call for judgment. With a 5-card major, balanced 15–17 HCP would normally have opened 1NT instead.*

## Responder's most common second action

| If opener rebids… | Weak/minimum responder | Stronger responder |
|---|---|---|
| **2♠** | **Pass** with about **6–9** points | **3♠** invites game (about **10–11**); **4♠** with game-going values |
| **1NT** | **Pass** with minimum balanced hand; **2♠** with about **6+ spades** and a weak hand | **2NT** invites game (about **10–11 HCP**); **3NT** with enough HCP for game |
| **2♥** | Often **Pass** with a minimum and reasonable heart tolerance; **2♠** with a long spade suit | **3♥** invites with heart support; **4♥** with game values and heart fit |
| **2♣ / 2♦** | **Pass** with a good fit for that minor; **2♥** to prefer opener's hearts; **2♠** with about **6+ spades** | **2NT** invites with stoppers (about **10–11 HCP**); **3 of an already-bid suit** often invites |
| **3♠** | **Pass** with a minimum | **4♠** accepts game invitation |
| **2NT** | **3NT** is usually the simple choice with an ordinary minimum hand | Choose **4♠** with an exceptionally long/good spade suit; explore more if needed |
| **3♥** | **Pass** with a minimum and heart tolerance | **4♥** with enough values and a fit |
| **3♣ / 3♦** | **Do not pass**: opener has forced to game | Find the best game (often **3NT**, **4♥**, or **4♠**) |

### Example auctions (opponents pass throughout)

1. **1♥ – 1♠ – 2♠ – Pass**: opener has a minimum with four spades; responder has only minimum values.
2. **1♥ – 1♠ – 2♠ – 4♠**: spade fit found; responder has enough to bid game.
3. **1♥ – 1♠ – 2♥ – Pass**: opener shows six or more hearts; responder is content with partscore.
4. **1♥ – 1♠ – 1NT – 2NT**: responder invites 3NT; opener passes with a minimum or bids 3NT with a maximum.

### Three rules to remember

1. **Raise partner's spades first** if you have a suitable fit; then show whether your hand is minimum, invitational, or very strong.
2. **A simple rebid of hearts shows extra length**, while **a jump in hearts shows extra strength**.
3. **Stayman and Jacoby transfers do not apply here.** They are used after a **notrump opening**, not after the 1♥ opening shown above.

**Source:** [ACBL SAYC System Booklet](https://web2.acbl.org/documentlibrary/play/SP3%20%28bk%29%20single%20pages.pdf), sections “Responses and Later Bidding” and “Subsequent Bidding by Responder.” Opening at 12 total points is our teaching modification, not strict SAYC.
